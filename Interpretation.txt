Recommendation 1
The Trousers landing experience deserves the highest priority for optimization
 because it consistently shows the highest customer engagement.

 Recommendation 2
 The Sale category also exhibits distinct browsing behaviour and should be 
 considered for future experiments.

 Recommendation 3
 Since Blouses and Skirts behave similarly, separate experiments for
 these two categories may offer limited additional insight. 
 They could initially be treated together when prioritizing experimentation.

 #    Does the position of the first viewed product influence customer engagement?
 #
 ############################################

 Business motivation:

If products displayed in certain positions receive consistently higher engagement,
 the company could evaluate its product placement 
strategy and consider controlled A/B experiments to optimize product ordering.

### Business Recommendation

Products initially viewed in the Top Left position exhibited the highest customer 
engagement among all six positions.
Although the overall effect size was small, repositioning products is typically 
a low-cost intervention. Therefore, the Top Left position represents a promising 
candidate for future controlled A/B experiments to determine whether 
the observed association reflects a causal improvement in customer engagement.
