### Session Behaviour

The dataset contains *24,026 browsing sessions* comprising *165,474 click events*.

Customers viewed an average of *6.89 products per session*, while the median session contained only *4 clicks*, indicating a right-skewed distribution with a relatively small number of highly active sessions.

This suggests that most visitors browse only a few products before leaving the website, whereas a minority explore many products.

### Initial Findings

- The dataset contains 165,474 click events from 24,026 browsing sessions.
- The Trousers category accounts for 30.06% of all click events.
- Skirts, Blouses and Sale each contribute approximately 23% of click events.
- Based on click counts alone, we cannot conclude that customers prefer trousers because product availability and page exposure are unknown.
